FAQ

Questions, answered

How does the bot join my meeting?

It joins from our servers like a normal participant, using the meeting link you paste. If the host has a waiting room, the bot waits to be admitted — just let it in like anyone else.

What if my meeting is already running?

Choose “Deploy right now” when creating the meeting — no date or time needed. The bot connects within moments.

Which platforms are supported?

Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams links are supported today.

Do meeting participants know it's there?

Yes — the bot appears in the participant list under a name you choose (e.g. “Mitaxy Notetaker”), so everyone knows the call is being recorded.

Can I share the notes with people who don't have an account?

Yes. Every completed meeting has a share option: anyone-with-the-link, or restricted to specific email addresses — each invitee gets a personal access link.

What happens if nobody admits the bot?

The bot waits patiently in the lobby. If it's never admitted, the meeting is marked failed with a clear explanation — you'll see exactly what happened on the dashboard.
